- **Normal flow and display** determine how boxes participate in layout before Flexbox or Grid.
- The **box model** consists of content, padding, border and margin.
- **`border-box`** makes declared dimensions include padding and borders, not margins.
- Prefer **content-driven sizing**, sensible limits and relative units over rigid fixed heights.
- Understand **margin collapsing**, **overflow**, and when floats are appropriate for text wrapping.
- Use **DevTools** to inspect actual box sizes before changing CSS.
