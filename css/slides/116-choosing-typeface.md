- Test legibility at the actual sizes used in the interface.

<!--
- Children’s learning platform: rounded letterforms such as Nunito or Comfortaa can support an approachable, playful identity. Use clear characters and avoid decorative fonts for instructions.
- Online newspaper: an editorial serif such as Georgia can reinforce a reading-oriented identity. Check paragraph measure, line height and comfort for longer articles.
- Wellness application: a humanist sans-serif such as Lato can work with muted colors, generous space and rounded components to suggest calmness.
- Compare these with a banking application or technical dashboard, where clarity and precise alignment may take priority. The same typeface could work in several contexts.
- Choose for the actual audience, content and task. Test small labels, ambiguous glyphs, language coverage and weights; a visually matching font is not automatically readable.
