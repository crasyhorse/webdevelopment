# Quiz — Flexbox

Work in groups of two. Each group receives exactly one of the six questions below. **Every experiment is independent:** use only your assigned folder; no results or files from another group are required.

## Instructions

1. Open your group’s `index.html` and `styles.css` in your editor. Open the HTML in a browser (or use Live Server).
2. Work through the experiments in your group README. Use DevTools or edit the CSS and reload.
3. Prepare a short explanation and demonstrate a change to the class. Both partners should be able to explain the result.
4. Do not open the answer slides until the group presentations are complete.

## Questions

1. **What is Flexbox and what is it used for?** — [Group 1: instructions and starter files](flexbox/01-what-is-flexbox/README.md)

2. **What are the main axis and cross axis, and how does `flex-direction` affect them?** — [Group 2: instructions and starter files](flexbox/02-axes/README.md)

3. **What does `justify-content` control?** — [Group 3: instructions and starter files](flexbox/03-justify-content/README.md)

4. **What is the difference between `align-items` and `align-self`?** — [Group 4: instructions and starter files](flexbox/04-alignment/README.md)

5. **What do `flex-wrap` and `gap` do?** — [Group 5: instructions and starter files](flexbox/05-wrap-gap/README.md)

6. **How do `flex-grow`, `flex-shrink` and `flex-basis` affect item sizes?** — [Group 6: instructions and starter files](flexbox/06-flex-sizing/README.md)
