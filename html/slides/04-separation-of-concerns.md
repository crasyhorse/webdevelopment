# Three technologies, distinct responsibilities

| **Technology** | **Responsibility** | **Question** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **HTML** | Structure **and meaning** | What is this content? |
| **CSS** | Presentation | How should it look? |
| **JavaScript** | Behavior | What should happen? |
