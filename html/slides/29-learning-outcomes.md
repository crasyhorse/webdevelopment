I will ...

1. learn how browsers turn **HTML and CSS into pixels**.
2. become familiar with the **DOM**, **CSSOM**, and render tree.
3. discover how **layout, paint, and compositing** contribute to rendering.
4. explore how browser DevTools help inspect **rendering behavior**.
