# Learning Outcomes

I will ...

1. learn why **native HTML elements** are the foundation of accessible interfaces.
2. become familiar with **roles and accessible names** in browser DevTools.
3. discover when **ARIA attributes** provide useful additional semantics.
4. learn how to distinguish **ARIA semantics** from built-in keyboard behavior.
